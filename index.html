The honoree

Ness B. Shroff

Research that connects theory, systems, and people.

Professor Shroff is an Ohio Eminent Scholar in Networking and Communications and a professor in both Electrical and Computer Engineering and Computer Science and Engineering at Ohio State. His work spans machine learning, network optimization, stochastic control, and the design and security of complex communication and computing systems.

He joined Ohio State in 2007 and leads the NSF AI Institute for Future Edge Networks and Distributed Intelligence, known as AI-EDGE. In 2026, he also directs Ohio State’s AI(X) Hub.

Workshop overview

NessFest 2026

NessFest is a one-day academic workshop bringing together colleagues, collaborators, students, alumni, and friends of Professor Ness B. Shroff for talks and conversations across the fields that have shaped his research community.

The workshop celebrates rigorous ideas, lasting collaborations, generous mentorship, and the people connected through them. It is being held in Professor Shroff’s honor on the occasion of his 60th birthday, while remaining first and foremost an academic gathering.
